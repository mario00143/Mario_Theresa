# Family & Helper Guide

A simple guide if someone invited you to help plan (or help run) the
wedding using WeddingOS. No technical background needed.

## Signing in

You'll get a link from whoever is organizing the workspace. Open it, sign
up with your email, and you're in. If you already have an account, just
sign in normally at the same web address.

## My Tasks

**Tasks** shows everything that needs doing. If tasks are assigned by
owner, you can filter to just yours. Tap a task to see details, add notes,
or mark it done.

## Guests

**Guests** shows the guest list, RSVP status, and any dietary or
accessibility notes. If your role allows editing, you can update a
guest's details or record their RSVP response.

## Transport

Under **Logistics**, you can see pickup/drop arrangements for guests —
who's arriving when, from where, and which vehicle/driver is assigned.

## Rooms

Also under **Logistics** — which guests are staying at which hotel, in
which room, and any special needs (extra bed, accessibility, etc.).

## Wedding Day Mode

On the actual wedding day, the app switches into a calmer, more focused
mode — go to **Wedding Day** and toggle **Wedding Day Mode** on from the
header. This keeps Emergency contacts one tap away and shows what's
happening right now instead of the full planning view.

## Live issues

If something goes wrong on the day (a vendor is late, an item is
missing), **Wedding Day → Issues** is where it gets logged so the right
person can act on it. Anyone with Day-of access can report one — you
don't need special permission to flag a problem.

## Emergency

**Wedding Day → Emergency** always has the hospital, ambulance, church,
hotel, and family emergency contacts, sorted by priority. This page stays
reachable even if your phone loses signal, as long as you refreshed the
Offline Pack at some point before you went offline.

## What not to edit

A few things are intentionally locked down to protect the couple's data
and the event's integrity:

- **Payments, refunds, and budget figures** — only Finance Lead, Couple,
  and Admin can see or change these. If your role doesn't show them,
  that's expected, not a bug.
- **Member roles and workspace settings** — only Admin/Couple.
- **Room and vehicle assignments beyond capacity** — the system itself
  blocks over-booking a room or vehicle past its capacity, so if an
  assignment fails with a capacity error, that's the system protecting
  against double-booking, not a glitch.
- **Deleting things** — be thoughtful before deleting a guest, task, or
  record. It's usually not the end of the world (a backup exists), but
  ask the Admin first if you're unsure whether something should be
  removed rather than just marked done/cancelled.

If anything looks locked or greyed out that you think you should be able
to edit, ask whoever set up your account to check your role in
**Settings → Members**.
